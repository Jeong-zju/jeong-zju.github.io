01Research direction

From pixels and words
to purposeful action.

My research sits at the intersection of robot manipulation, embodied intelligence, and multimodal learning. I develop generalizable robot policies for complex, long-horizon tasks.

I’m currently a Computer Science PhD student at The University of Sydney, advised by Prof. Weiming (William) Zhi.
